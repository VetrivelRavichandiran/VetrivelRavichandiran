## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hello! I'm **Vetrivel Ravichandiran**, a final-year Electronics and Communication Engineering student at the **Central University of Karnataka, India**.

I enjoy exploring how electronics, communication systems, and intelligent software can work together to solve real-world engineering problems.

My interests span RF and antenna engineering, wireless communications, signal processing, embedded systems, and AI-driven applications. I enjoy moving beyond theoretical concepts by developing software prototypes, experimenting with machine learning, and learning from industrial environments.

- 🎓 **Education:** B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering
- 📡 **Core interests:** RF engineering, antenna design, wireless communications and signal processing
- 🧠 **AI & software:** Machine learning, computer vision, predictive analytics and backend development
- 🔌 **Hardware:** Embedded systems, microcontrollers and electronics manufacturing
- 🏭 **Industry exposure:** Nokia, Samsung, Aptiv, Foxlink and UCAL
- 🎯 **Career interests:** Wireless/RF engineering, semiconductor and electronics engineering, embedded systems, and AI-enabled engineering

> My goal is to build engineering systems where communication technology, intelligent algorithms, and practical implementation come together.
